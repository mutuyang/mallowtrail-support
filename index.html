Good to know

Little questions, answered.

How do I play?

Place one friend in each row, each column, and each colored region. Friends must not touch, including at a corner. A distant diagonal is fine. Tap a square to mark it, double-tap to place or remove a friend, and drag to mark several squares. Tap the board with two fingers to undo the last move.

What comes with each trail pack?

The first 30 main levels, Mochi, and daily puzzles are free. Little Trail opens 200 levels, three companions, and two camp gifts. Full Trail opens all 500 levels, five companions, five camp gifts, and four extra expressions. Little Trail owners see the upgrade price on the Full Trail card. Each pack is a one-time purchase and includes unlimited hints. The purchase screen shows the price for your App Store region.

I bought a pack. How do I access my levels?

Main levels open in order as you finish the preceding level. To recover missing access, connect to the internet with the Apple Account you used to buy the pack and open Settings → Restore purchases. If access is still missing, email us with the app version and what you see. Keep Apple Account credentials and payment-card details private.

How do I move my progress to another device?

Use Restore purchases to recover access to your packs. To move your puzzle progress, export a backup from the old device, transfer the file using a service you choose, and import it on the new device. Importing replaces the current progress after confirmation. Your device's system backup may also include the game data.

How do Rules and Hint work?

Rules explains how to play. Hint highlights a helpful next move. Free players get two hints per UTC day. Both trail packs include unlimited hints. Daily puzzles have three difficulty options.

Can I play offline?

Downloaded game content and saved progress work offline. Purchasing, checking store availability, or restoring an Apple purchase requires a connection. The first version includes a daily puzzle schedule through December 12, 2027; later dates will need a content update.

Where do I request a refund?

Apple handles App Store billing and refund decisions. Visit Apple's billing and purchase support for help with a charge. Deleting the app does not remove your Apple purchase history.

The app is quiet, slow, or behaving unexpectedly.

Check Sound and Music in Settings, device volume, and silent mode. For an unexpected problem, tell us your iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, app version, and the level you were playing. A screenshot and short description are helpful. Export a backup before reinstalling, because deleting the app can remove local progress.
