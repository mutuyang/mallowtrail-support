Good to know

Little questions, answered.

How do I play?

Place one friend in each row, each column, and each colored region. Friends must not touch, including at a corner. A distant diagonal is fine. Tap a square to mark it, double-tap to place or remove a friend, and drag to mark several squares. Tap the board with two fingers to undo the last move. Long-press placement is also available in Settings.

What is included in the full adventure?

The first 20 main levels are free. The one-time full-game purchase unlocks the complete 120-level adventure and three woodland camps. It is not a subscription. The purchase screen shows the price for your App Store region. The app contains no advertising.

I bought the game, but a level is still locked.

Make sure your device uses the same Apple Account used for the purchase and has an internet connection. Open Settings and choose Restore purchases. You can also restore from The full adventure screen. If access still does not return, email us with your app version and what you see. Never send your Apple Account password or payment-card details.

Does restoring a purchase restore my progress?

Restoring a purchase restores access to the full game. Puzzle progress is saved separately on your device. Mallowtrail does not run its own cloud sync service. To move progress, export a backup from the old device, transfer that file using a service you choose, and import it on the new device. Importing replaces the current progress after confirmation. Your device's system backup may also include the game data.

How do Explain and Kitty Hint work?

Explain offers the rules and logical guidance without a daily limit. Kitty Hint highlights a helpful next spot and is available twice per UTC day. Daily puzzles have three difficulty options. There are no countdown timers or paid hint packs.

Can I play offline?

Downloaded game content and saved progress work offline. Purchasing, checking store availability, or restoring an Apple purchase requires a connection. The first version includes a daily puzzle schedule through December 12, 2027; later dates will need a content update.

Where do I request a refund?

Apple handles App Store billing and refund decisions. Visit Apple's billing and purchase support for help with a charge. Deleting the app does not remove your Apple purchase history.

The app is quiet, slow, or behaving unexpectedly.

Check Sound and Music in Settings, device volume, and silent mode. For an unexpected problem, tell us your iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, app version, and the level you were playing. A screenshot and short description are helpful. Export a backup before reinstalling, because deleting the app can remove local progress.
