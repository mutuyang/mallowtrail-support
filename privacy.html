Privacy policy

Effective November 16, 2026 · Mallowtrail for iPhone and iPad

Your trail belongs to you. Mallowtrail does not require a game account, run advertisements, or include a third-party analytics SDK.

The game stores progress on your device. It does not send your puzzle notes, camp progress, settings, or gameplay history to a server operated by the developer.

Who this policy covers

This policy describes Mallowtrail and its official support website. The developer can be contacted at mutuyang@gmail.com. Apple and the providers described below operate their services under their own privacy policies.

Game progress and device storage

The app saves your puzzle progress, marks, unlocked companions, camp improvements, memories, preferences, and daily hint usage locally. A previous valid save is kept as a recovery copy. Your device backup may include these files depending on your Apple backup settings.

You can export a progress file and import a previously saved file using the system file picker. You decide where that file goes and who receives it. Mallowtrail does not provide its own cloud synchronization service or automatically upload exported files.

Purchases handled by Apple

Apple processes optional trail pack purchases and refunds. Mallowtrail uses StoreKit to verify and restore access on your device. Apple's processing of store, account, payment, and platform data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Permissions and tracking

Mallowtrail does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, or location. Importing or exporting a backup gives access only through the system file interface you choose. The app contains no advertising SDK, cross-app tracking, or third-party analytics SDK.

If you contact support

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever information you choose to include, such as a description, screenshot, or device summary. We use this information to answer your request and investigate the reported issue. Support correspondence is handled through Gmail; see Google's Privacy Policy.

Please do not send payment-card information, passwords, or unrelated personal information. We keep correspondence only as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable obligations. You can ask us to access, correct, or delete correspondence by emailing the address above. We do not sell your support information or use it for advertising.

This support website

This is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not use analytics scripts, advertising, web forms, or cookies set by us. GitHub records visitors' IP addresses for security purposes when serving GitHub Pages. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. Following an external link or sending an email also uses the services of the destination provider.

Your choices

  • Keep play local, and choose whether to purchase or contact support.
  • Export a backup from Settings when you want a portable copy of progress.
  • Delete the app to remove its local data. Manage any exported copies or system backups separately.
  • Manage purchase history, refunds, and Apple Account information through Apple. Deleting the app does not cancel or erase an Apple purchase.
  • Contact us about information you voluntarily supplied to support. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to object to processing or contact your local data-protection authority.

Children

The game does not collect personal information through gameplay. If you believe a child has sent personal information to our support email and would like it removed, please contact us. Apple manages purchase permissions and family controls for Apple Accounts.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy if a future version changes how information is handled. The effective date will be shown on this page. For privacy questions, contact mutuyang@gmail.com.
